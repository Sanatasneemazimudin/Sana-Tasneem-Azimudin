# 💫 About Me

👯 I’m looking to collaborate on projects involving **AI/ML, Software-Defined Networking, distributed systems, or full-stack web development**.

🤝 I’m looking for opportunities to contribute to **research-oriented AI and networking projects**, including areas such as federated learning and edge AI architectures.

🌱 I’m currently learning **Linear Algebra for Machine Learning and Quantum Computing** through NPTEL/SWAYAM.

💬 Ask me about **Python, SDN, Mininet, Ryu, scikit-learn, TensorFlow, or MERN stack development**.

⚡ Fun fact: Collaborating with great teammates and leveraging AI tools, I’ve worked on projects ranging from a decentralized satellite swarm simulator (**ORBIT v2.0**) to a deepfake and privacy risk detection engine (**MIRAGE**) and a disaster-preparedness game (**Tiny Planet**).
